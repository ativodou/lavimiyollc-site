INSTITUTIONAL PARTNERSHIP

For those working to solve Haiti for generations.

Fèl an Kreyòl Inc. is a Florida nonprofit with a single mission: getting Creole books into the hands of Haitian students who have never had them. If your organization is trying to change Haiti's educational trajectory, this is the conversation we need to have.

THE PROBLEM

220 years. Not one technical book in their language.

Haiti has 12 million Creole speakers. For 220 years — since independence — there was not a single technical, legal, or educational book published in their native language. Mechanics, electricians, plumbers, lawyers, judges — all forced to learn in French, a language spoken by fewer than 10% of the population. The language was never the barrier. The books were missing.

THE SOLUTION

119 volumes. 9 series. First time in history.

The Lavi Miyò National Library is the first complete technical, legal, scientific, and educational library ever produced in Haitian Creole. 6 legal codes (4,000+ articles). 6 technical series covering automotive, electrical, plumbing, electronics, carpentry, and masonry. 15 volumes on science, history, and mathematics. 4 bilingual dictionaries.

HOW IT WORKS

A model built to outlast any single donor.

The books already exist. Fèl an Kreyòl Inc. does not ask partners to fund the creation of content — the content is done. We ask partners to fund distribution: getting books that already exist into schools that need them. Your contribution is a direct, auditable transaction. Books purchased. Schools documented. Zero overhead.

WHY NOW

A generational moment for change.

Haiti's educational infrastructure faces unprecedented pressure. The window to establish a Creole-language knowledge base — before another generation graduates without it — is open now. The library exists. The distribution model is ready. What is missing is the partnership network to scale it.
